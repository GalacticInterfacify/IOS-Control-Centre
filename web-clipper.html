Export

  1. Get the file onto the device and open it in Safari (AirDrop, email attachment, or a local web server).
  2. iOS hands it to Settings → General → VPN & Device Management. Older versions show “Profile Downloaded” at the top of Settings instead.
  3. Tap Install, enter the passcode, confirm. The icon lands on the home screen.
  4. The profile is unsigned, so iOS flags it as “Not Signed” — expected for locally built clips.
